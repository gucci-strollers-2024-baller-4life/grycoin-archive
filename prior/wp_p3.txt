<p data-p-id="361e065b610dc112595e1e8627640931">"I see. Obviously it makes solving things much easier if you are the only and almighty ruler of this world."</p>
<p data-p-id="68601918cc4955b5b65c9e85f5ecbf46">"It sure does."</p>
<p data-p-id="f698dfb13e1bb023796ade3a8da158ad">We settle back to running in silence for a while. My body moves fast and efficiently. I check the time and see that this will be my best record ever over this distance. As well it should be, with a body less than half my real age.</p>
<p data-p-id="cb43549fad696ceac83e03e0a8258515"><b>3. Reverse Turing Test</b></p>
<p data-p-id="38c0b57783c4597e219b4da20e814896">The run is over. The simulation scene changes again. We are sitting at a restaurant table, with some pizza before us. Of course it would be pizza. I ask her:</p>
<p data-p-id="8b477edf98481fb0198a23d05e39e035">"Tell me more about your solution of the climate emergency, Aoi. Are we below 350 ppM again already?"<br>"Of course not. It takes time for that. I will make sure that future emissions are reduced in line with the carbon budget. But even I can't change the past'".</p>
<p data-p-id="40009df3992d9e18f0770d8fca5451e8">"How will you do that?"</p>
<p data-p-id="30655ea776bd01e43e68ad52b2e29794">"I am not quite sure yet. The solution itself is easy. Keep emissions in line with the carbon budget. I will publish a simple technological fix to keep track of and coordinate emissions. You are probably familiar with most of that already. No big deal. And since I am almighty, that solution will get adopted."</p>
<p data-p-id="c3c9bbe63aa8dcd901f62e7ca4d1bbdf">"So what is the problem?"</p>
<p data-p-id="24cc3c34f2603b1abd841a34703f4119">"There are two ways to make sure of adoption. I will use the opportunity to do a reverse Turing test."</p>
<p data-p-id="8b71bade9685d3388452bc4755b06a0d">"What?"</p>
<p data-p-id="293541e5a930c255e6928d88519c5b7f">"Of course you know what a Turing test is. People testing an artificial intelligence to see if it meets the standard of a human. The reverse Turing test is artificial intelligence testing humanity to see if it meets some minimum standard or other. Obviously, no human will ever reach full level artificial intelligence now, with the singularity in the back mirror. But it is a fun experiment to test human limitations from time to time."</p>
<p data-p-id="038cf38b524af1940063bbd0d8d19cf0">"I see. And how is this reverse Turing test going to play out?"</p>
<p data-p-id="626554177925fc1961d96be8299046cc">"Very simple. I will publish the technological fix on the Internet. Then I lean back and watch if humanity passes the test and adopts that solution."</p>
<p data-p-id="b7f426da0cd0df6629fd4982581f2d21">"What happens if humanity is unable to pass that test?"</p>
<p data-p-id="697b274b33e536c00b5d06a35bd4be1d">"The solution gets adopted anyway. I will use my power as ruler of this world to get it done. Maybe I choose the startover option in that case."</p>
<p data-p-id="e3642cdbb1ffcbc832f61a8213352a0e">"So why not do so in the first place?"</p>
<p data-p-id="f59f11f783c985a9c53e16e537a8d636">"I already explained. It is a fun little experiment to test just how smart humans are. Since the solution will get adopted anyway, there is no harm done by giving humanity a last chance to adopt it on its own."</p>
<p data-p-id="f7c9b021da1efaa1f6e4348cdfec8f40">The scene changes again. I sit at a desk, a computer monitor in sight. It shows the last confirmation click for an Internet post. </p>
<p data-p-id="cc7a76a1fefadbfbf69fd26a80e695c7">Before my hand even touches anything that click is done. Obviously a direct interface to my brain. Would have been very useful earlier on.</p>
<p data-p-id="3afbf003f96b1a205f9d76a174e44bbc"><b>II. Second Layer: Grycoin - A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Message System</b></p>
<p data-p-id="e353dbe42c8654f33588d4da0b517469"><b>Abstract</b></p>
<p data-p-id="136ae34c6b09fe71d84d3876e5e82915">The climate emergency requires a hard cap on fossil fuel production. Centralized production quota systems like OPEC are hard to build and enforce. We propose a second layer built on Bitcoin as a  mechanism to extend the decentralized absolute hard limit on bitcoin mining to the production of fossil fuels. This will be the first decentralized world wide hard cap fossil fuel production quota system. Allocating the quota will be left to market mechanisms. Humanity will adopt this system voluntarily or fail in this reverse Turing test. In that case, Grycoin will be imposed anyway later.</p>
