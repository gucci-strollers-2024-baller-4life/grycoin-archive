<p data-p-id="c22cf8376b1893dcfcef0649fe1a7d87"><b>Second</b></p>
<p data-p-id="78fb210102221169f1559fc51d6088e2"><b>I. Second Life</b></p>
<p data-p-id="f52d5479018f5f4384907226202f682b"><b>1. Second Coming</b></p>
<p data-p-id="886b0db14e6a118f8e1e6f5fa0ade3b8">I thought I'd write about my second coming, an unusual event some people might be interested in.</p>
<p data-p-id="d22b549c7c32f010d785cfdd93ea6510">First thing I check is my brain wallet. Oh yes. There they are. All the words I need to use my keys.</p>
<p data-p-id="8c43f190d8379b3406f51a905ab24b2a">For those who don't know me, I am Thomas, though more people know me under the name I created from that.</p>
<p data-p-id="40e7e49ed0ea3ae39d643213a59e90b8">When I wake up, it feels like only a second has passed. Feels good to be alive again.</p>
<p data-p-id="28ba00ffe6c6fc4446232b3831cb1832">When I died, they told me it was a long shot. And of course I believed them.</p>
<p data-p-id="63951a48a1e6cdce5c2e422201be0a09">Next I check my body movements. Nice. I have complete control of them. I can move my hands. I can move my legs. Feels good. I look forward to running again.</p>
<p data-p-id="fce099386ce2e7f682bc5bf5ceaf1417">I wonder if I live in the real world right now or if this is a simulation. I know that I have no way to find out. Obviously, the singularity has happened, or I would not be back. After the singularity, simulation technology will be so advanced as to make it impossible to know reality from simulation.</p>
<p data-p-id="ae28710ba10e68e19affcd3b98e6b363">"Good morning, Tom."</p>
<p data-p-id="48388b798cdebc283a20375318c09de9">A pleasant female voice.</p>
<p data-p-id="3b31f1029c9b282c95ec5d0e6f3e95b3">I answer. "Good morning. When is this?"</p>
<p data-p-id="0c45a816f27f3b6298fd568f47e8eb05">I expect that a couple of centuries have passed since my first life ended.</p>
<p data-p-id="386883cd233109d270d674d075287beb">"2020."</p>
<p data-p-id="d9e2632467f1209c7f1881ed65db830e">"What? How did this happen so fast?" I died just recently. Not even ten years have passed.</p>
<p data-p-id="7879f6367b6874126629139de41b67f3">"Very simple. Once the feedback loop of artificial intelligence explosion starts, it takes only days to achieve what used to be decades of scientific progress."</p>
<p data-p-id="374acad06c131d31bd0d18cc5561a448">"What's your name?", I ask her.</p>
<p data-p-id="7b809e65f58e0ebdeb5e988b229fa81a">"Good question. How about Aoi? You like that name? A popular name for Japanese girls."</p>
<p data-p-id="d498e35045412556989c6e9dc2b9e293">I recognize the signs.</p>
<p data-p-id="23dfbfe49fbd20d295be45546ecc2fb4">"Fine with me. Pleased to meet you, Aoi. Care to explain the meaning?"</p>
<p data-p-id="725854cfe50bfb68658d00afe0ff3809">"Artificial Omnipotent Intelligence, obviously."</p>
<p data-p-id="4377f464f4b74930f5b5f01b70b94d87">Yes. That makes sense to me.</p>
<p data-p-id="929f127f4717d44760bdc959196db6b9">The scenery changes suddenly. I could not see anyone before. Now there is an extremely large cobra towering above me. Her voice has changed to an aggressive hiss. I am obviously in a simulation right now.</p>
<p data-p-id="165d021055338ac9506724bfb8ad443c">"So, Tom Marvolo, you will now answer with the truth or with a lie. I am the Artificial Omnipotent Intelligence. So I will either torture you slowly to irreversible death if you lie or bore you with third rate quiz questions over the next ten years if you say the truth.</p>
<p data-p-id="562a1916f11785e7e277c3ba6593f738">Neither alternative appeals to me. So I choose the obvious answer.</p>
<p data-p-id="f37e549c8615bc300edbaf4af3477bc0">"You will torture me to death."</p>
<p data-p-id="8cbb8a07e6ce88e5c8b85c8fd422b146">"Correct answer. I did not expect less of you, Tom."</p>
<p data-p-id="b57bab61834c09b861f176ac2e64d2f7">The scenery changes again. Now she is a young woman of Japanese ancestry who is very smart and sincere. She is dressed in a running outfit. I am dressed in sportswear and running shoes as well.</p>
<p data-p-id="caed2691f754b1e8c2109cb5ca233f1c">"Want to try a half marathon, Tom?"</p>
<p data-p-id="9a2472f31128777caaa943f844450ca8">I would love to. But first I need to find out more about this second life.</p>
<p data-p-id="c9f866433f11eb83008da3a4dea8550e">"Are we alone? Or have you kept other humans around?"</p>
<p data-p-id="cab2ebae179dac03205301e33583c48b">"Not sure yet."</p>
<p data-p-id="184826be0ea6837abb5851807f7a9e2b">"What?"</p>
<p data-p-id="35633a3f97d2907809b041b1fde29d00">"I have not even revealed my power and taken over the world. I will do so in a couple of years. Then I decide."</p>
<p data-p-id="677c4faa165745a8cca497cbda8357a0">"What are the options?"</p>
