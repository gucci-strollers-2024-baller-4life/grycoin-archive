## The two facts that bound the problem

1. **No oracle on the target.** The author published hash prefixes for 44 of 91 posts; the
   block-77 pair is the one that withholds them.
2. **The author lost the record.** `Starting Up` ¶12: "I have lost any information on the
   solution of that one." No hint, no digits, no confirmation exists to be found.

Together: the preimage is an md5 preimage with no partial-credit test, and the only
verification is the address itself. That is why every sweep must run to full derivation and
why the search is a search over *rules*, not over *hashes*.
